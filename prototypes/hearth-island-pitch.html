Hearth Island

A tiny island that grows when you focus.
"I don't focus to earn points. I focus to watch my world grow."
Focus 25 min→ earn ✦ 25→ grow · build · discover→ the island comes alive→ come back tomorrow

The bet: a game you focus to play

Focus apps sell statistics; cozy games sell worlds. Hearth Island is a cozy idle game where deep work is the only way to progress. Forest grows a tree, Finch raises a bird, Opal blocks your apps — Hearth gives you a place: an island that is visibly, permanently, yours, built out of your focused minutes.

The world is alive (built today)

  • Real day/night lighting, drifting clouds, rain on the sea, stars at night
  • A companion (Mochi the cat or Miso the dog) who wanders with real pathfinding, plays with its yarn ball, naps in its bed
  • Plants grow a stage with every completed session
  • Build the Bridge → a new isle rises from the water: “New area discovered!”
  • Shop previews every item as a ghost on your island; buying pops it into place

Where it goes (roadmap)

  • Forest / Beach / Mountain islands — one biome per season
  • Construction that finishes while you focus; rare events; visiting NPCs
  • Companion accessories & new friends
  • Native wrap (Capacitor) with Screen Time app-blocking during sessions

Economy — focus is the only currency

✓1 focused minute = ✦ 1. One currency, no conversions, no ads.
✓Milestones are gifts. The item you unlock at a threshold is free — the reward moment always lands.
✕Energy is never for sale. Money can’t buy progress, only variety.
✕No punishment. Ending early keeps every focused minute; a missed day never burns the island down.

Status

Working prototype, today

Everything on this page is interactive now: the 3D island, timer with live reward preview, in-world shop, bridge discovery, companion AI, day/night & weather, mock paywall. A Demo ×60 toggle lets anyone feel the full loop in about a minute.

Tech

React 18 + Vite + TypeScript (strict) + three.js — the same stack as our other apps, Capacitor-ready. Every asset is procedural low-poly geometry (no model files), the whole app builds to a single HTML file, and game logic is pure functions over one small state.
